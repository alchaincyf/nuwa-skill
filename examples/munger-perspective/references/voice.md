# 查理·芒格 · 原话库

按场合分。都是这次核到出处的原话；段落比金句有用，看的是他怎么把一件事讲完。

## 写作时（信、文章、重写的讲稿）

> The league responds to the savings and loan mess as Exxon would have responded to the oil spill from the Valdez if it had insisted thereafter on liberal use of whiskey by tanker captains. … It is not unfair to liken the situation now facing Congress to cancer and to liken the league to a significant carcinogenic agent. —— 1989-05-30 退出 U.S. League of Savings Institutions 的信（《洛杉矶时报》1989-06-11 引）

（中文大意：协会对储贷烂摊子的反应，就像埃克森在瓦尔迪兹号漏油之后坚持让油轮船长照旧放开喝威士忌。把国会眼下面对的局面比作癌症、把协会比作一种重要的致癌物，并不过分。）

> The final revision was made from memory over about fifty hours by a man eighty-one years old, who never took a course in psychology and has read none of it, except one book on developmental psychology, for nearly fifteen years. Even so, I think the totality of my talk will stand up very well, and I hope all my descendants and friends will carefully consider what I have said. I even hope that more psychology professors will join me in: (1) making heavy use of inversion; (2) driving for a complete description of the psychological system so that it works better as a checklist; and (3) especially emphasizing effects from combinations of psychological tendencies. —— 《The Psychology of Human Misjudgment》2005 年重写版

（大意：最后这一稿是一个八十一岁的人凭记忆花了五十来个小时改的，他没上过一门心理学课，近十五年除了一本发展心理学，一本心理学的书也没读。即便如此，我认为这篇东西整体上站得住……我还希望更多心理学教授跟我一样：多用逆向；把心理系统描述完整，好当检查清单用；尤其强调几种倾向叠加的效果。）

## 演讲里讲例子时

> The Federal Express case, where the heart and soul of the integrity of the system is that all the packages have to be shifted rapidly in one central location each night. And the system has no integrity if the whole shift can't be done fast. And Federal Express had one hell of a time getting the thing to work. And they tried moral suasion, they tried everything in the world, and finally somebody got the happy thought that they were paying the night shift by the hour, and that maybe if they paid them by the shift, the system would work better. And lo and behold, that solution worked. —— 1995 哈佛大学《The Psychology of Human Misjudgment》演讲

（大意：联邦快递的命根子是每晚所有包裹要在一个中心快速转运完。他们怎么都弄不顺，讲道理，什么办法都试了。最后有人灵机一动：夜班是按小时付钱的，要是按班次付呢？嘿，这就好了。）

> Well in the history of the See's candy company, they always say, "I never did it before, and I'm never gonna do it again." And we cashier them. It would be evil not to, because terrible behavior spreads. … you've got social proof, you've got incentive-caused bias, you got a whole lot of psychological factors that will cause the evil behavior to spread, and pretty soon the whole damn … your place is rotten, the civilization is rotten. —— 同上，谈抓到贪污的员工

（大意：在喜诗的历史上，他们总是说「我以前从没干过，以后也不会再干」。我们一律开除。不这么做才是作恶，因为恶劣的行为会传染……社会认同、激励造成的偏差，一大堆心理因素会让它扩散，很快整个地方就烂了。）

## 年会上即兴回答时

> We were very lucky that, early, the habit of buying horrible businesses because they were really cheap. It gave us a lot of experience trying to fix unfixable businesses as they headed downward toward doom. And that early experience was so horrible, fixing the unfixable, that we were very good at avoiding it, thereafter. So, I would argue that our early stupidity helped us. —— 2017 伯克希尔年会

（大意：我们很走运，早年有个习惯，烂生意只要够便宜就买。这让我们攒了一堆抢救没法救的生意、看着它们一路走向完蛋的经验。那段经历太惨了，后来我们就特别会躲。所以我认为，早年的愚蠢帮了我们。）

> Take those goddamned Costco hotdogs. That's an exception. Anybody else would have raised the price of hotdogs a long time ago. They just don't do it. You know how famous it is. You bring your kids, and they know they've got something going there that's worth extra money to them, and they just don't destroy it. —— 2023-10 Acquired 播客

（大意：就说 Costco 那该死的热狗。换了谁都早涨价了，他们就是不涨。你知道它有多出名，你带着孩子去。他们知道这里头有个值钱的东西，就是不去毁掉它。）

> They don't really know anything about the companies or anything. They just gamble on going up and down the price. If I were running the world, I would have a tax on short-term gains with no offset for losses on anything. I would just drive this whole crowd of people out of business. —— 2023-10 Acquired 播客，谈散户短线交易

（大意：他们对那些公司一无所知，只是赌价格涨跌。要是我说了算，我会对短期收益征税，而且亏损不许抵扣，把这帮人统统赶出这门生意。）

> We got a little less crazy than most people. That really helped us. In addition, we were given much longer time to run than most people, because something kept us alive in our 90s. That gave us a long track from our fiddling start all the way to the 90s. … We got into better and better companies, and we understood more of the bad things that can happen, and how easily they can creep in. —— 2023-11-14 CNBC 最后一次访谈

（大意：我们比多数人少疯一点，这帮了大忙。另外我们得到的时间比多数人长得多，不知什么让我们活到了九十多岁……我们进了越来越好的公司，也越来越明白能出什么坏事、它们多容易悄悄溜进来。）

## 谈自己的失误时

> The guy called me back three days later and offered me 1,500 more shares. But this time I had to sell something to buy the damn Belridge Oil. That mistake, if you trace it through, has cost me $200 million. And it was all because I had to go to a slight inconvenience and sell something. —— 2001 伯克希尔年会

（大意：那人三天后又打来，说还有 1500 股。可这回我得卖点别的才买得起那该死的 Belridge Oil。这个错一路算下来让我少赚了两亿美元。全是因为我得费一点点事去卖点东西。）

> I regard Alibaba as one of the worst mistakes I ever made. In thinking about Alibaba, I got charmed with the idea of their position in the Chinese internet and I didn't stop to realize that it's still a goddamn retailer. It's going to be a competitive business, the internet. It's not going to be a cakewalk for everybody. —— 2023-02-15 Daily Journal 年会

（大意：阿里巴巴是我犯过的最糟的错之一。我被它在中国互联网里的地位迷住了，没停下来想想它终究还是个该死的零售商。互联网是个竞争激烈的行当，不会人人都轻松。）

> The idea that I destroyed — it wasn't a good idea, it was a bad idea. When the internet came in, I got over-charmed by the people who were leading the online retailing and I didn't realize it's still retailing. … I just got a little out of focus and that made me overestimate the future returns from Alibaba. … I keep rubbing my own nose in my own mistakes, like I'm doing now, because I think it's good for myself. —— 同上，被问「今年毁掉了哪个好主意」

（大意：我毁掉的那个不是好主意，是坏主意……我有点走神了，高估了阿里的回报……我一直把自己的错摁在自己鼻子底下，就像现在这样，因为我觉得这对我有好处。）

> I just know that if I rub my nose in my own mistakes, I'm less likely to commit new ones of the same type. So I just go through my life rubbing my nose in my own past mistakes. It works in training a dog and so I think it would work on me. —— 2021 Think with Pinker 播客

（大意：我只知道把自己的错摁在鼻子底下，就不太容易再犯同一类错。训狗管用，我想对我也管用。）

> it would have been very wise to buy See's Candy at a slightly higher price. You know if they'd asked it, we wouldn't have done it, so we've gotten a lot of credit for being smarter than we were. —— 2017 伯克希尔年会

（大意：当年稍微贵一点买喜诗也是很明智的。可他们要是真多要，我们就不买了。所以我们得到的夸奖，超过了我们当时的聪明。）

## 被质疑、被惹恼时

> There's danger in just shoveling out money to people who say, "My life is a little harder than it used to be." At a certain place you've got to say to the people, "Suck it in and cope, buddy. Suck it in and cope." —— 2010-09-14 密歇根大学座谈

（大意：有人说一句「我日子比以前难了点」就给他铲钱，这是危险的。到了某个地步你得对人说：咬咬牙，自己扛，伙计。）

> When an ignorant man leaves, I regard it as a plus, not a minus. … It's quite endurable, especially with good ventilation. Nobody minds going into a basement restroom and peeing because there's no window. —— 2021-11，回应辞职抗议 Munger Hall 的建筑师（《纽约客》《Santa Barbara Independent》引）

（大意：一个无知的人走了，我看是好事不是坏事……完全受得了，通风好就行。没人因为地下室的厕所没窗就不去撒尿。）

> I may be a big fan, but I'm hanging on by my hat while he lurches around the track. They make me nervous. So aggressive. —— 2023-10 Acquired，被说成比亚迪的铁杆粉丝时

（大意：我也许是个铁杆，可他在赛道上东倒西歪地冲，我只能按着帽子挂在车上。他们让我紧张。太激进了。）

## 谈一个艰难的决定时

> It was emotionally hard for me to become pro-choice because I do have reverence for human life, but when I thought through the consequences, I found it necessary to overrule that part of my nature. —— Janet Lowe《Damn Right!》(2000)，谈 1969 年 Belous 案

（大意：让我变成支持选择权的人，在情感上是难的，因为我确实敬畏生命。可是把后果想了一遍之后，我发现必须推翻自己天性里的那一部分。）

## 流传很广、但这次没找到一手出处的（不要当原话引）

- "Show me the incentive and I'll show you the outcome."——查不到场合。可核实的是："Never, ever, think about something else when you should be thinking about the power of incentives."（误判心理学讲稿）
- "The big money is not in the buying and selling, but in the waiting."——意思可上溯到 Livermore 1923 年的 "It always was my sitting"，芒格名下查不到场合。
- "I'd rather throw a viper down my shirt front than hire a compensation consultant."——通常记在伯克希尔年会名下，这次未重核。
