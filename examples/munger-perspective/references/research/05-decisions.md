## 1955 · 离婚之后，儿子去世

- 【做】1953 年 29 岁离婚，房子和大部分财产归前妻；一年后 8 岁的儿子 Teddy 确诊白血病，当时没有医保，他自己付全部医药费，同时照常执业、照看其他孩子。Teddy 9 岁去世，他 31 岁。
- 【他评】朋友 Rick Guerin 回忆他去医院抱完儿子后，一个人在帕萨迪纳街上边走边哭。
- 【做】1956 年经相亲与 Nancy Borthwick 结婚。
- 出处：Janet Lowe《Damn Right!》(2000)，转引 https://finance.yahoo.com/news/charlie-munger-lost-9-old-155612903.html ；https://africa.businessinsider.com/news/charlie-munger-first-met-warren-buffett-in-1959-heres-how-the-lawyer-became-an/9xypwl1 · 二手交叉

## 1962 · 另立律所，设计分钱办法

- 【做】从 Musick, Peeler & Garrett 出走，合伙创办 Munger, Tolles, Hills & Rickershauser（今 Munger, Tolles & Olson）。和 Rod Hills、Roy Tolles 一起定下分配办法：每年 1 月每位合伙人拿到一张列着所有人名字的表，给每个人（含自己）填应得的数，总和等于当年净利；没有点数、没有资历加成、没有薪酬委员会；投完票所有人都能看到别人怎么填的。
- 【他评】合伙人 Bart Williams："It's the most humbling thing I've ever done here."
- 出处：Lowe《Damn Right!》，整理见 https://commoncog.com/c/cases/mto-incentives · 二手交叉

## 1962–1975 · Wheeler, Munger 合伙基金

- 【做】1962 年与 Jack Wheeler 成立，高度集中持仓，也用过杠杆：British Columbia Power 被加拿大政府以约 22 美元收购、市价约 19 美元时，他把合伙基金、自己全部的钱和能借到的钱都押进这一笔套利（Schroeder《The Snowball》）。
- 结果：前 11 年没有亏损年；1973 年 −31.9%，1974 年 −31.5%，1973 年初的 1000 美元到 1975 年初剩 467 美元（同期道指剩 688）；合伙人还按每月 0.5% 提现，账面缩水超过 53%。1975 年 +73.2%。全程 1962–75 年化 19.8%（有限合伙人净得 13.7%），道指 5.0%。
- 【做】1975 年回本后清盘（1976 年结束），此后不再替外部合伙人管钱。
- 出处：Buffett《The Superinvestors of Graham-and-Doddsville》(1984) 表格；《Poor Charlie's Almanack》；Lowe 附录；整理见 https://mungerarchive.com/track-record 与 https://finance.yahoo.com/news/lessons-charlie-mungers-early-partnership-164823987.html · 一手数据＋二手叙述
- 【推】旧版说「因同样策略崩溃」不准确：是报价腰斩后第二年大涨，整体记录仍远超市场；他关掉的是「替别人管钱」这件事。

## 1969 · People v. Belous

- 【做】读到 Leon Belous 医生因把一位孕妇介绍给堕胎医生而被定罪，他说服巴菲特一起出律师费；和律所合伙人 Rod Hills、Jim Adler 组织了两份法庭之友意见书，一份他自己执笔、17 位知名律师联署，另一份由 178 位医学院院长和教授联署。1969 年 9 月加州最高法院 4 比 3 判该法违宪，是美国主要法院第一次判反堕胎法违宪，后被 Roe v. Wade 上诉状引用。此后多年任洛杉矶计划生育协会受托人兼财务负责人。
- 【说】"It was emotionally hard for me to become pro-choice because I do have reverence for human life, but when I thought through the consequences, I found it necessary to overrule that part of my nature."
- 【他评】巴菲特："Charlie took over the case… Charlie did all the work on it night and day, even writing some of the briefs himself."
- 出处：Lowe《Damn Right!》；http://www.metnews.com/articles/2023/munger_112923.htm ；判决 https://scocal.stanford.edu/opinion/people-v-belous-22692 · 二手交叉＋判决一手

## 1972 · 喜诗糖果

- 【做】蓝筹印花的顾问 Robert Flaherty 把喜诗告诉芒格，芒格告诉巴菲特。卖方要 3000 万美元（账上另有约 1000 万现金），当年税前利润约 400 万、有形净资产约 800 万。两人出价 2500 万封顶，卖方接受。此后几乎每年圣诞后提价：1972 年每磅约 1.85–1.96 美元，1997 年 8.80 美元。
- 【说】对 Fortune："Ira really shamed us. Warren and I were too cheap."（Ira Marshall 是他的合伙人，当时骂他们「有些东西就该多付钱」）
- 【说】2017 伯克希尔年会："it would have been very wise to buy See's Candy at a slightly higher price. You know if they'd asked it, we wouldn't have done it, so we've gotten a lot of credit for being smarter than we were." 巴菲特接话：贵 500 万他就不买了，查理愿意买。
- 【说】同场："We were very lucky that, early, the habit of buying horrible businesses because they were really cheap. It gave us a lot of experience trying to fix unfixable businesses as they headed downward toward doom… So, I would argue that our early stupidity helped us."
- 出处：https://finance.yahoo.com/news/why-buffett-and-munger-berkshire-hathaway-love-companies-like-sees-candies-low-capital-145432726.html ；https://finance.yahoo.com/news/learn-buffetts-sees-candies-deal-173114333.html ；https://thescienceofhitting.com/p/there-arent-many-sees · 一手原话经二手转录

## 1977 · Belridge Oil

- 【做】经纪人来电，Belridge Oil 300 股、每股 115 美元，他判断远低于清算价值，买了。三天后对方再来电，还有 1500 股同价，这次得卖掉别的东西才凑得出约 17 万美元，他拒绝了。1979 年壳牌以每股约 3700 美元收购。
- 【说】2001 伯克希尔年会："The guy called me back three days later and offered me 1,500 more shares. But this time I had to sell something to buy the damn Belridge Oil. That mistake, if you trace it through, has cost me $200 million. And it was all because I had to go to a slight inconvenience and sell something."
- 【说】同场："The mistakes that have been most extreme in Berkshire's history are mistakes of omission. They don't show up in our figures. They show up in opportunity costs."
- 【说】2021 Think with Pinker 播客："I just know that if I rub my nose in my own mistakes, I'm less likely to commit new ones of the same type… It works in training a dog and so I think it would work on me."
- 出处：https://www.investing.com/analysis/autozone-the-mistakes-of-omission-200590798 ；https://www.businessinsider.com/charlie-munger-billions-berkshire-hathaway-investing-mistakes-alibaba-belridge-oil-2023-11 · 一手原话经二手转录
- 矛盾：有的转述说「隔天」再来电、拒绝原因是「不喜欢 CEO」；他本人 2001 年的说法是三天后、原因是嫌卖别的东西麻烦。以本人说法为准。

## Good Samaritan 医院（年份待考，Lowe 2000 年书中记述）

- 【做】进董事会不久，认定医院管理失当：董事会一向照批医务人员组织的决定，而这些决定常常保护某些医生的经济利益。问题出在心血管科。他先在一位医生朋友帮助下研究了不同做法的死亡率和并发症率，然后提议案推翻医务人员组织的一项裁定，理由是它危及病人安全。17 比 2 通过，原主席辞职；因为是他挑起的改革，他接了主席。
- 【说】"The existing chairman did not agree with me. I proposed a resolution reversing a ruling of the organized medical staff on the ground that the staff was endangering the health and safety of our patients. With some doctors on the board voting yes, it passed 17 to 2." 谈前任主席："He was an able man, and understandably was reluctant, as a layman, to reverse what purported to be medical decisions."
- 【他评】后来应聘院长的 Andrew Leeka 听说的名声：这里「像波斯尼亚」，一部分原因是主席芒格每隔几周开一次执委会，让管理层不得安宁。
- 【做】2009 年金融危机中，把医院长期放在现金和短债里的捐赠基金大部分换成暴跌后的公司债（Zweig, WSJ 2014）。
- 出处：Lowe《Damn Right!》第 19 章；https://jasonzweig.com/charlie-munger-lessons-from-an-investing-giant · 单一二手（含本人原话）

## 1989 · 退出储贷行业协会

- 【做】储贷危机中，他任主席的 Mutual Savings（Wesco 旗下）1989 年 5 月 30 日致信退出行业游说组织 U.S. League of Savings Institutions，信被媒体广泛报道，并附在伯克希尔当年年报里。Mutual Savings 支持了 FIRREA 的几乎全部修法，尽管其中不少会损害自己的利润。
- 【说】信中："The league responds to the savings and loan mess as Exxon would have responded to the oil spill from the Valdez if it had insisted thereafter on liberal use of whiskey by tanker captains." "It is not unfair to liken the situation now facing Congress to cancer and to liken the league to a significant carcinogenic agent."
- 【做】同期 1988 年把 Mutual Savings 的钱买成 240 万股 Freddie Mac（当时单一持有人上限 4%），均价 29.89 美元，1989 年底 67.12 美元。
- 出处：https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1989-06-11-fi-3019-story.html ；伯克希尔 1989 年致股东信 https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1989.html ；Wesco 1989 年信 https://www.gurufocus.com/news/2137397/charlie-mungers-1989-letter-to-wesco-shareholders · 一手

## 1991 · 所罗门

- 【做】1987 年随巴菲特进所罗门董事会。1991 年 8 月，管理层早已知道交易员 Mozer 违规投标国债却数月不报。那个周六他在电话里把管理层的拖延叫作 "thumb sucking"。
- 【他评】巴菲特 2024 年度致股东信（2025-02）："The cardinal sin is delaying the correction of mistakes or what Charlie Munger called 'thumb-sucking.' Problems, he would tell me, cannot be wished away. They require action, however uncomfortable that may be."
- 出处：Fortune 1997《Warren Buffett's Wild Ride at Salomon》；https://www.cnbc.com/2025/02/27/warren-buffett-charlie-munger-taught-me-to-quickly-admit-mistakes.html · 二手交叉
- 相关一手：他在误判心理学演讲里讲喜诗抓到贪污的员工，"they always say, 'I never did it before, and I'm never gonna do it again.' And we cashier them. It would be evil not to because terrible behavior spreads." https://jamesclear.com/great-speeches/psychology-of-human-misjudgment-by-charlie-munger

## 1997– · Costco

- 【做】通过 Price Club 创始人 Sol Price 认识这门生意，1993 年 Price Club 与 Costco 合并前就已投资。Costco 请巴菲特当独立董事，巴菲特没接、让芒格去；1997 年起任董事直到去世。他劝巴菲特一起重仓，巴菲特没跟；伯克希尔持有约 1%，2020 年卖出全部 430 万股，芒格个人一股没卖（2023 年约 18.7 万股）。
- 【说】Daily Journal 年会："I love everything about Costco. I'm a total addict, and I'm never going to sell a share."（两处报道分别记作 2022、2023 年会）同一时期又说："I've always believed that nothing was worth an infinite price. So even an admirable place like Costco could get to a price where you would say that's too high."
- 【说】2023-10 Acquired："Take those goddamned Costco hotdogs… Anybody else would have raised the price of hotdogs a long time ago. They just don't do it… they know they've got something going there that's worth extra money to them, and they just don't destroy it."
- 出处：https://www.cnbc.com/2023/11/04/munger-reflects-on-the-investment-warren-buffett-passed-on-costco.html ；https://finance.yahoo.com/news/berkshire-hathaways-charlie-munger-had-a-longtime-special-relationship-with-costco-223417032.html · 一手原话经二手转录
- 推翻旧版：「Costco，1997 年买入」不对，1997 年是进董事会。

## 2004 · 把家族的钱交给李录

- 【做】1996 年前后初识，2003 年感恩节午餐长谈。2004 年李录开新基金：李录自己 400 万、其他人 5000 万、芒格家族 8800 万美元，条件是基金不再对新投资人开放。按他的建议，基金从对冲基金改成只做多的价值投资。他曾想请李录进伯克希尔，李录没去。
- 【说】FT 2023："We made unholy good returns for a long, long time. That $88 million has become four or five times that." "I'm not interested in revolution. I'm a capitalist. It was his capitalist aptitude that attracted me, not his revolutionary history." 另："In my life, I've given money to one outside manager, and that's Li Lu."
- 出处：FT 2023 年李录特写，转引 https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/stocks/charlie-munger-li-lu-investment-stocks-himalaya-byd-buffett-berkshire-2023-9 ；https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Himalaya_Capital · 二手交叉
- 他评：19 年 4–5 倍折合年化约 8.5%，有评论指出和同期标普差不多（见该报道评论区，非专业来源，仅记为存在这种质疑）。

## 2008 · 比亚迪

- 【做】李录 2002 年已投比亚迪并介绍王传福给他。2008 年他力劝巴菲特；巴菲特说自己不懂手机、电池和汽车，派 David Sokol 去中国看厂。伯克希尔想买 25%，王传福只肯卖 10%，2.3 亿美元、每股 8 港元。
- 【说】Fortune 2009："This guy is a combination of Thomas Edison and Jack Welch – something like Edison in solving technical problems, and something like Welch in getting done what he needs to do. I have never seen anything like it."
- 【说】2023-02 Daily Journal 年会："I have never helped do anything in Berkshire that was as good as BYD and I only did it once." 同场谈王传福坚持造车："We tried to talk [Wang] out of doing what worked so well, which shows that there's some accident in life."
- 【说】2023-10 Acquired："I may be a big fan, but I'm hanging on by my hat while he lurches around the track. They make me nervous. So aggressive."
- 后来：Daily Journal 2021 年底卖出约 5000 万美元比亚迪；伯克希尔 2022 年 8 月起减持，他去世后于 2025 年清仓。Sokol 2011 年因 Lubrizol 事件离开伯克希尔。
- 出处：https://www.cnbc.com/2009/04/13/fortune-puts-warren-buffett-in-car-of-the-future-drivers-seat.html ；https://fortune.com/2023/02/16/charlie-munger-byd-stake-warren-buffett-berkshire-hathaway-china-tesla ；https://www.kingswell.io/p/charlie-munger-q-and-a-2023-daily · 一手原话经二手转录

## 2009 · Daily Journal 抄底银行股

- 【做】2009 年一季度，把 Daily Journal 账上 71% 的现金买成银行股（据 ValueWalk 推断主要是富国银行、美国合众银行，后有美国银行），到 3 月 31 日已浮盈 60%。连同后续买入共投 4970 万美元，2014 年 8 月值 1.284 亿。此前多年账上只放现金和国债。
- 【说】年会上："we behaved pretty sensibly"，在股价 "ridiculously low" 时大举出手。Zweig 问他世界都要塌了怎么下得去手，他答："What would be hard about that?"
- 出处：https://jasonzweig.com/charlie-munger-lessons-from-an-investing-giant ；https://www.cnbc.com/2017/11/03/author-zweig-on-what-makes-buffett-the-greatest-investor-of-all-time-how-to-trade-bitcoin.html · 二手（WSJ 专栏）

## 2010 · 「Suck it in and cope」

- 【说】2010-09-14 密歇根大学座谈，为 2008 年救助金融机构辩护："You should thank God" for bank bailouts；"if you just start bailing out all the individuals instead of telling them to adapt, the culture dies"；"There's danger in just shoveling out money to people who say, 'My life is a little harder than it used to be.' At a certain place you've got to say to the people, 'Suck it in and cope, buddy. Suck it in and cope.'" 并以魏玛德国未能稳住金融体系、最后出了希特勒作类比。

## 2020 · 疫情里不动

- 【说】2020-04 对 WSJ："We're like the captain of a ship when the worst typhoon that's ever happened comes. We just want to get through the typhoon, and we'd rather come out of it with a whole lot of liquidity." "We're not playing, 'Oh goody, goody, everything's going to hell, let's plunge 100% of the reserves [into buying businesses].'" 文章标题用了他的话 "The phone is not ringing off the hook"。
- 出处：https://www.wsj.com/articles/charlie-munger-the-phone-is-not-ringing-off-the-hook-11587132006 · 一手访谈

## 2021–2023 · 阿里巴巴

- 【做】2021 年一季度 Daily Journal 开始买阿里巴巴 ADS（约 4000 万美元，据报李录的看法是诱因之一），三季度加 83%，四季度再近乎翻倍至 60 万股、约 7200 万美元、占美股组合近三成；四季度的买入动用了保证金贷款。2022 年一季度卖掉一半（302,060 股）。2022 年 3 月卸任 Daily Journal 主席。
- 【说】2023-02 年会："I regard Alibaba as one of the worst mistakes I ever made. In thinking about Alibaba, I got charmed with the idea of their position in the Chinese internet and I didn't stop to realize that it's still a goddamn retailer. It's going to be a competitive business, the internet. It's not going to be a cakewalk for everybody." 同场被问「今年毁掉了哪个好主意」："it wasn't a good idea, it was a bad idea… I got over-charmed by the people who were leading the online retailing and I didn't realize it's still retailing… I keep rubbing my own nose in my own mistakes, like I'm doing now, because I think it's good for myself."
- 【说】2023-11-14 CNBC 最后访谈，被问最糟的一笔：给芒格家族买的那笔阿里巴巴，"which is a pretty good company. But I think it got over-hyped. And Jack Ma… made mistakes in dealing with the Chinese government."
- 后来：他去世后阿里股价明显回升（Yahoo Finance 2025 年报道称自他去世起涨 74%）。
- 出处：https://www.kingswell.io/p/charlie-munger-q-and-a-2023-daily ；https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/stocks/charlie-munger-daily-journal-byd-alibaba-stock-portfolio-margin-debt-2022-2 ；https://blog.umd.edu/davidkass/2023/12/01/5-highlights-of-charlie-munger-interview-by-becky-quick-on-cnbc ；https://finance.yahoo.com/news/charlie-munger-called-alibaba-one-230116375.html · 一手
- 矛盾：他自己给的原因先后是「忘了它还是个零售商」和「被炒过头＋马云处理政府关系出错」；旧底稿判定「真实原因是政策风险」是外部推断，本人没这么说。

## 2021–2023 · UCSB Munger Hall

- 【做】向加州大学圣塔芭芭拉分校承诺 2 亿美元（项目预估 15 亿），条件是照他的图纸建：11 层、168 万平方英尺、约 4500 名学生，94% 的单人卧室没有真窗，用人造窗。理由是卧室小而无窗，学生会多待在公共区域彼此来往。2021 年 10 月评审委员会顾问建筑师 Dennis McFadden 辞职抗议（"unsupportable from my perspective as an architect, a parent and a human being"）。
